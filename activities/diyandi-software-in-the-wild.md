## 1\. User group

**Who are you designing for?**  
Visitors of Mugna Fiesta Park during the Diyandi Festival, particularly those who are unfamiliar with the stalls and their locations.

**Why might this group need support during Diyandi?**  
Mugna offers a variety of food, clothing, accessories, and other products from different vendors. With many stalls to explore, visitors may need assistance in finding stores or products that match their interests.

\---

## 2\. Situation or need

**What is this group trying to do during Diyandi?**  
Visitors want to discover different stalls, explore available products, compare prices, and locate stores they are interested in visiting.

\---

## 3\. Problem or inconvenience

**What may make this task difficult, confusing, unsafe, slow, or inconvenient?**  
With the number of stalls in Mugna, visitors may have to walk around the entire area just to find a particular product or store. This can be tiring and time-consuming, especially when the place is crowded. Visitors may also overlook stalls that offer products they are interested in.

\---

## 4\. Proposed digital solution

**What digital tool would you propose?**  
I propose a **Mugna Stall Discovery Kiosk**, an interactive touchscreen kiosk placed near the entrances and other accessible areas of Mugna. It would serve as a digital directory containing stall names, product categories, menus or product catalogs, price ranges, and an interactive map of the area.

**How would it help the intended users?**  
The kiosk would allow visitors to explore available stalls and products without having to walk around the entire area first. Once they find a stall they are interested in, they can view its location on the map. It could also help smaller businesses gain visibility among visitors.

\---

## 5\. Things users can do

1. Search and filter stalls based on product categories, keywords, and price ranges.
2. Select a stall to view its products, prices, and location on an interactive map.

\---

## 6\. Important qualities

### Quality 1: Ease of Use

**Why does this matter to users?**  
Visitors of different ages and levels of familiarity with technology should be able to use the kiosk without difficulty. The interface should have clear labels, readable text, and simple navigation.

### Quality 2: Reliability

**Why does this matter to users?**  
Stall locations, products, and prices should be accurate and regularly updated. Outdated information may lead visitors to the wrong stall or create confusion about available products.

\---

## 7\. How to tell whether the solution helped

Visitors could be asked to locate a particular stall with and without using the kiosk. The time taken to find the stall could then be compared. Feedback could also be collected to determine whether the kiosk made discovering and locating stalls easier.
